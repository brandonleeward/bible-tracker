# Version 2.0 Roadmap

## Features & Upgrades

- [ ] **UX & Layout Overhaul**: Polish the interface to make it "pop". Fix alignment issues (off-center buttons/text), refine button layouts, and elevate the overall aesthetic to feel deeply premium.
- [ ] **Theme Customization & Dark Mode**: The app currently adapts to system dark mode, but we will add explicit theme selectors (e.g., Light, Dark, Sepia) and custom color accents.
- [ ] **Data, Studies, & Motivation**: Integrate insights and data (like the Center for Bible Engagement's "Power of 4" study showing the impact of reading 4+ times a week) to educate and motivate users. 
- [ ] **Encouraging Verses**: Display rotating, carefully selected Bible verses throughout the app to encourage users to stay in the Word.
- [ ] **Badges & Achievements**: A robust milestone system tracking your lifetime metrics. Gamification of the app. Earn badges for total chapters read, total books completed, various streaks, total days read, and hitting the "4 times a week" consistency goal, etc.
- [ ] **"Continue Reading" Quick-Action**: Enhance the Continue Reading button so that it immediately displays a popup or screen with the next few unread chapters in your most recently active book (e.g., if you last read 2 Chronicles 29, it surfaces chapters 30, 31, and 32), allowing you to check them off without navigating the full books list.
- [ ] **Streak Back-filling (Date-Only)**: Enhance historical backdating to allow users to log that they read on a specific past date without having to specify *which* chapters they read. This is perfect for users porting over data from external habit trackers to rebuild their streaks.
- [ ] **Reading History / Activity Timeline**: A timeline view showing exactly which chapters were read on specific dates, utilizing the timestamps already being captured in the database.
- [ ] **Historical Back-dating (Chapters)**: Enhance the "Sync Past Progress" tool to allow users to select a specific past date when bulk-completing books.
- [ ] **Cloud Sync & User Accounts**: Allow users to create an account to back up their progress across devices. 
- [ ] **Custom Reading Plans**: Allow users to create structured, non-linear reading plans (e.g., "Chronological", "M'Cheyne", or "Bible in a Year").
- [ ] **Reminders & Notifications**: Local push notifications to remind the user to read at a specific time of day.
- [ ] **Reading Statistics & Analytics**: Enhance the "Statistics" screen with more in-depth data, graphs, and insights (e.g., reading speed, time spent reading, most read books, etc.).
- [ ] **Offline-First & Performance**: Ensure the app is fully functional offline, with fast load times and smooth performance.
- [ ] **Home Screen Widget**: For Android and iOS devices, allow users to add a widget to their home screen to quickly access the app and view their progress.
